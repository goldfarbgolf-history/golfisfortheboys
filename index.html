Welcome to the Archive

The Goldfarb Golf trip — affectionately known as GG — has been a near-annual tradition since 1997. From the original 1997 Myrtle Beach run to recent stops in Wilmington (2023), Myrtle Beach (2024) and Kingsmill (2025), this site is a living archive of every trip, every course, every dubious wager.

Data shown reflects records compiled from group history (1997-2021) and merged 18Birdies data for Mort's rounds (2021, 2023, 2024, 2025). Other players' 2022-2025 scores will be added as exports become available.
